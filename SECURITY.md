# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.**

Report it privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting: go to the
affected repository, open the **Security** tab, and choose **Report a vulnerability**.
If that is unavailable to you, email **friday@theaimatrix.io** with `SECURITY` in
the subject line.

Please include:

- what the issue is and where it lives (repository, file, endpoint)
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- the impact you believe it has
- any suggested fix

## What to expect

| Stage | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledgement of your report | within 3 business days |
| Initial assessment and severity | within 7 business days |
| Fix or mitigation for high severity | within 30 days |

We will keep you updated while we work, and we will credit you in the release
notes once a fix ships, unless you would rather stay anonymous.

## Scope

In scope: source code, build and deployment configuration, and dependencies in
repositories owned by the `baicoders` organization.

Out of scope: third-party services we do not operate, social engineering,
physical attacks, denial of service by volume, and reports generated by an
automated scanner with no demonstrated impact.

## Handling

Do not access, modify or retain data that is not yours while investigating.
Please give us a reasonable window to ship a fix before disclosing publicly.
