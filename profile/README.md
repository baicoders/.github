# baicoders

We build practical software for small businesses and institutions — point of
sale, back-office, and the APIs behind them.

### What we work on

- **Retail systems** — offline-first point of sale, inventory and stock ledgers,
  purchase orders, and receipts.
- **Institutional APIs** — centralized services for schools and co-ops.

### How we work

Every change reaches `main` through a reviewed pull request. Our
[contributing guide](https://github.com/baicoders/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
covers the branch, review and merge rules, and our
[security policy](https://github.com/baicoders/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
covers how to report a vulnerability privately.
